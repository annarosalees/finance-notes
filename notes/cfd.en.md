# CFD Notes

🇯🇵 [日本語版](./cfd.md)

Notes on CFDs (Contracts for Difference), organized from the angle of
how they work and how they're handled in practice. Examples covered:
indices (Japan 225), commodities (WTI crude oil, gold), US stocks,
ETFs, and more.

> These notes grew long, so they're split by theme into five files.
> Reading them in order takes you from how CFDs work through to how
> they're handled in practice.
